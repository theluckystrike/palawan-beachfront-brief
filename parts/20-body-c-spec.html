How to read every fact here

Nothing on this page is stated without a confidence level. If a claim has no source you can click, it is labelled as such and you should treat it as a rumour until an office confirms it.

Verified Reported Inferred Unverified Kill
  • Verified means two independent sources, or one authoritative document from the office that issues it.
  • Reported means one listing or one page said so. A seller saying a lot is titled is a reported claim, not a fact.
  • Inferred means I worked it out from something else, and the basis is stated next to it.
  • Kill means the lot fails a rule that cannot be negotiated.
